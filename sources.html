Medical Sources

Citations for PAL Aid first aid guidance

All first aid guidance in PAL Aid is based on established clinical guidelines from recognised medical authorities in the United Kingdom and internationally. We do not provide medical diagnoses, only evidence based first aid information.

NHS (National Health Service)

nhs.uk/conditions/first-aid ↗
Resuscitation Council UK

⚠️ PAL Aid provides general first aid information only. This information is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
